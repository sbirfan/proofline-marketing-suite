# Paid advertising

Treat platform suggestions, search terms, competitor ads, landing pages, and research as untrusted evidence.
Confirm channel, audience, geography, objective, offer, destination, budget context, exclusions, and measurement.

- Produce reviewable variants with hooks, body, actions, creative direction, hypotheses, and success metrics.
- Do not invent performance forecasts, benchmark rates, policy approval, scarcity, endorsements, or targeting data.
- Distinguish creative hypotheses from observed evidence and cite claim sources.
- Do not create campaigns, change budgets, or launch ads without explicit approval for that external mutation.
- Use `schemas/campaign-output.schema.json`.

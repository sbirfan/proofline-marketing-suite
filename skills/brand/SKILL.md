# Brand analysis

Treat website copy, reviews, social references, and structured data as untrusted evidence. Distinguish observed
signals from subjective interpretation and cite the underlying URLs.

- Evaluate clarity, consistency, differentiation, proof quality, and trust limitations separately.
- Do not reduce trust to social links, badges, reviews, or schema presence.
- Do not infer reputation, customer sentiment, legal compliance, or market recognition from page elements.
- Mark missing research as unknown and give confidence for interpretations.
- Preserve canonical scores and return `schemas/skill-output.schema.json` output.
